# ATS Myths Busted

*Source: [The Tech Resume - ATS Myths Busted](https://thetechresume.com/samples/ats-myths-busted)*

## Common ATS Misconceptions

### Myth 1: ATSes Automatically Reject Resumes
**Reality:** Humans, not systems, review and make decisions about applications. As recruiter Amy Miller states: "The list of people in an ATS is organized by date when they applied. A real-life person then makes a decision."

### Myth 2: PDFs Are Not ATS-Compatible  
**Reality:** Both PDF and Word documents are parsed effectively. Recruiters actually recommend PDFs to preserve formatting.

### Myth 3: ATSes Use Advanced AI to Filter Candidates
**Reality:** Most ATS systems are basic. They primarily help track application status and do not automatically eliminate candidates. The idea of a "genius, AI-infused tool" is "ridiculous."

### Myth 4: You Need to "Optimize" Your Resume for ATS
**Reality:** There's no special "ATS-compatible" resume format. The key is tailoring your resume's content to the specific job description.

### Myth 5: Automated Screening Eliminates Most Resumes
**Reality:** Recruiters manually review applications. The only automated filtering typically occurs through specific "knockout questions" set by recruiters.

## Key Takeaway

Focus on creating a clear, relevant resume that highlights your skills and experience. Recruiters want to quickly understand your potential fit for the role, not decode an "ATS-optimized" document.

## Practical Implications

- Use either PDF or Word format - both work fine
- Focus on content relevance over formatting tricks  
- Tailor your resume to match job requirements
- Keep formatting clean and readable for human reviewers
- Don't sacrifice readability for perceived "ATS optimization"
